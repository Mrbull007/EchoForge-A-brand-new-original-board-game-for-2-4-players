# EchoForge-A-brand-new-original-board-game-for-2-4-players
EchoForge – A brand-new original board game for 2–4 players
Game Overview
EchoForge is a fast-paced, strategic race-and-resource game set in a fractured timeline. You are an Echo Smith, a master craftsman who travels through time, gathering Echo Shards from different eras to forge powerful Relics. The board is a 5×5 Timeline Nexus. The first player to forge 3 Relics and return to the central Forge Core wins.
Playtime: 20–40 minutes
Ages: 10+
Perfect for families, friends, or quick digital sessions.
1. Components (physical version)

1 × 5×5 board (Timeline Nexus)
4 player tokens (🔴 Red, 🔵 Blue, 🟢 Green, 🟣 Purple)
Echo Shards (4 types):
🟦 Ancient (blue)
🟩 Medieval (green)
🟧 Industrial (orange)
🟪 Future (purple)
5 Relic types (see below)
1 six-sided die

2. Setup

Place the 5×5 board.
Each player picks a token and places it on their starting corner:
Red → top-left (row 1, col A)
Blue → top-right
Green → bottom-left
Purple → bottom-right

The Forge Core is the exact center square.
Era Nodes (fixed locations) automatically grant shards when landed on.
Portals and Hazard squares are marked.
Players start with 0 shards and 0 forged relics.

3. How to Play (Turn Structure)
Players take turns clockwise. On your turn:
Step 1 – Roll the Die
Roll 1d6. This is your Movement Points (1–6).
Step 2 – Move
Move your token any number of spaces up to the roll (you may move fewer).

Movement is orthogonal only (up, down, left, right).
Manhattan distance rule: |Δrow| + |Δcol| ≤ roll.
You may pass through any square, including other players.
Multiple tokens can occupy the same square.

Step 3 – Resolve the Square You Land On

Era Node → Gain 1 shard of that era’s type (unlimited supply).
Portal → After landing, you may teleport for free to any other Portal square (bonus action).
Hazard → Lose 1 random shard from your inventory (if you have any).
Forge Core → You may forge relics here (see below).

Step 4 – Forge Relics (anytime after moving)
You can forge as many relics as you can afford in one turn.
Each relic has specific shard costs

Step 5 – End Turn
Pass to the next player.
4. Winning the Game
At the end of your turn, if you have 3 or more forged relics AND your token is on the Forge Core, you win immediately!
Ties are impossible because turns are sequential. If the game drags, the player with the most forged relics after 20 full rounds wins (rare).
5. Special Rules

You cannot stay completely still if you rolled ≥1 (you must move at least 1 space or use portals cleverly).
No direct player attacking – it’s a race of efficiency and timing.
Portals are extremely powerful for quick returns to the center.
Hazards punish hoarding too many shards without forging.

6. Strategy Tips

Balance collecting all four shard types for Eternal Flame.
Use portals to rush back to the Forge Core once you have enough shards.
Timing your arrival at the Core with exactly 3 relics is key – don’t get stranded with 2 relics and no movement left!
